# 7 Conclusion (draft 2)

An experiment that leaves sites unmeasured can certify every action. On each level of the plan one decision is taken, after a uniform draw from a level fixed before it: each skipped site falls under a claim, the share of the level skipped under a false claim is bounded, and each measured site carries the reason it was measured; the order fit, freeze, draw, calibrate, decide keeps each draw exchangeable with its level whatever came before. On three fully measured datasets the record matched the guarantees, with informed and random scores, natural-neighbour and Gaussian-process interpolation and three forms of guarantee. The model set the cost of a guarantee; trusted on its own uncertainty, the same model stated a rate that was sometimes right and sometimes several times too low. The certified claim decided what a guarantee bought: where measurements were taken, where wrong skips gathered and which sites were protected. The claim therefore belongs to the scientific question and enters the contract with it, before the experiment. A held-out audit certified the finished map of any campaign, and a label-conditional bound protected the sites of interest. Optimisation through level sets, scores that rank sites by the information they add to a process model, a model of beam time, and deployment at autonomous instruments, beginning with a synthesis campaign, are natural next steps. Each skipped site leaves the campaign with a reason and a claim, each level with a bound, and the record allows every decision to be replayed.
