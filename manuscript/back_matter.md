# Data availability (draft 1)

The NaCl microdiffraction scan is available from Dryad (doi:10.5061/dryad.p2ngf1w4t) [ALSNaCl]. The following are deposited on Zenodo (doi:10.5281/zenodo.XXXXXXX, reserved): the infrared map of the bioreef sample as recorded and after scattering correction, with the hierarchical design used here; the forward model of the synthetic scattering map and its design; the forward model of the simulated calcination campaign, with its design, the records of its 30 worlds and the kinetic fits; the run record of all 36 configurations, 250 campaigns each, with the decision and reason for every design site, the repeated calibration draws and the analyses of SI 6; and a snapshot of the software. The software provides the hierarchical design, natural-neighbour and Gaussian-process interpolation, the scores, the three forms of guarantee and the conditional rate, the map bound, the Monte Carlo acceptance check for new forms, the scattering correction for infrared maps, and the scripts that regenerate every figure and table from the record (SI 4).
